This policy explains what information Cash Paws handles, why, and the choices
you have.

## What stays on your phone

Your game progress (level, coins, cats, daily tasks, Bell Quest, settings,
and a record of coin packs already delivered) is stored only on your device. We have no server of our own and no account
system, so there is nothing to sign in to and no copy of your progress
anywhere else. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is gone.

## Reminders

If you allow notifications, Cash Paws can remind you when your Rewards Box is
ready and when a new Bell Quest is available. These reminders are scheduled
on your phone by the app itself. No information is sent anywhere to create
them. You can turn them off in the game (Settings → Reminders) or in your
phone's notification settings.

## Gameplay statistics and crash reports

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, Cash Paws uses
**Google Firebase**: Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics.
They receive:

- gameplay events, such as levels reached and lost, how long a level took,
  Sorts and Undos used, coins earned and spent in the game, daily tasks,
  Rewards Boxes, Bell Quest progress, and reminder settings;
- advertising events: which ads were offered, shown or watched, the ad
  revenue each one earned, and technical errors when an ad fails to load;
- coin pack purchases: which pack, its price and currency (Google Analytics
  for Firebase records these automatically for apps sold through Google Play);
- an app-instance identifier that Firebase creates for this installation (it
  is not your name, email or phone number, and resets if you reinstall), and
  your device's advertising ID, which links ad revenue to gameplay;
- device information, such as model, operating system, language and app
  version, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your
  IP address;
- crash reports and error details if the app crashes or misbehaves.

We use this only to improve the game: to see where levels are too hard, which
features are used, how ads perform, and what is going wrong. We do not use it
to identify you. Google processes it for us under its terms:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

**Game settings (Firebase Remote Config).** When it opens, the game downloads
its current settings from Firebase, such as how difficult levels are, how much
help it gives when you're stuck, and how often ads appear. To find the
settings that make the game most enjoyable, we sometimes give different groups
of players different settings and compare how each group plays, using the
statistics above. This uses the same app-instance identifier; no other
information is collected for it.

Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically.

## Advertising

Cash Paws is free and supported by ads. There are two kinds:

- **Optional ads** that you choose to watch for an in-game reward, such as a
  free Sort when you are stuck, or opening the Rewards Box early.
- **Ad breaks.** Past level 10, a short ad plays at the end of each level,
  announced first by an "Ad break" screen.

Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**. To have ads ready when needed, the app
requests them from Google in the background while you play, not only at the
moment one is shown. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect and
process information from your device, such as:

- your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
- device information, such as model, operating system and language;
- your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
- information about the ads you see and interact with;
- technical and diagnostic information about ad performance.

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and, where
permitted, to show ads based on your interests. How Google handles this
information is described in its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not sell your information for money.

## Backups with Google Play Games

If you use **Google Play Games** on your phone, Cash Paws signs you in to it
automatically and keeps a copy of your game progress in your Google account
(Play Games "Saved Games"). This lets you continue on a new phone or after
reinstalling. The copy is stored by Google; we have no server and never see
it. The app shows your Play Games name in Settings and keeps it on your phone.
To stop backups, sign out of Play Games or remove Cash Paws from your Play
Games account. How Google handles this: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Ratings and sharing

After a few levels the game may show Google Play's own rating sheet. Whether
it appears, and what you write, is handled by Google; the app is not told
whether you rated. Sharing opens your phone's share menu; what you share goes
only to the app and people you choose.

## Purchases

Cash Paws can be played in full without paying. Optional **coin packs** can be
bought in the Shop. They are sold and paid for through **Google Play**:

- Google handles the payment. We never see your card, bank, UPI or other
  payment details, or your Google account.
- Google Play tells the app which pack was bought, with an order reference,
  so the coins can be added. The app keeps that reference on your phone so a
  purchase is never delivered twice.
- Google processes your purchase under the Google Play terms and the Google
  Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Coins have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. Because your
  progress is kept on your phone (and, if you use Play Games, backed up to
  your Google account), coins are lost if you uninstall without a backup.
- Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. For a problem with a purchase,
  contact us with the order number from your Google Play receipt.

## Privacy choices and consent

Where the law requires it, Cash Paws uses Google's consent tool (the User
Messaging Platform) to show you a privacy message before personalised ads are
used, and to record your choice. Which message you see, if any, depends on
where you are.

## Your choices

- **Interest-based ads.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt
  out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings under Google → Ads (or
  Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone). You will still see ads, but they
  will not be based on your interests.
- **United States residents.** Some state laws treat sharing device data for
  interest-based advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.
  You can opt out in the game: **Settings → Do not sell or share my personal
  information**, which opens Google's privacy choices. This option appears
  where Google's consent tool determines it applies to you.
- **Reminders.** Sent only if you allow notifications; switch them off any
  time in Settings.
- **Stop everything.** Uninstalling the app stops all collection. To have
  statistics already sent deleted, contact us at pixelartgames000@gmail.com
  with the approximate date you installed the game.

## India

If you are in India, you may contact us about how your personal data is
handled under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including to
access, correct or erase it, or to raise a grievance, at pixelartgames000@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

## Children

Cash Paws is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow
children's personal information to be collected. If you believe a child has
used the app and had data collected by our ad or analytics partners, contact
us at pixelartgames000@gmail.com and we will ask for it to be deleted.
