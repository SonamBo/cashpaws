## What stays on your phone

Your game progress (level, coins, cats, daily tasks, Bell Quest and settings)
is stored only on your device. We have no server of our own and no account
system, so there is nothing to sign in to and no copy of your progress
anywhere else. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is gone.

## Reminders

If you allow notifications, «App name» can remind you when your Rewards Box is
ready and when a new Bell Quest is available. These reminders are scheduled
on your phone by the app itself. No information is sent anywhere to create
them. You can turn them off in the game (Settings → Reminders) or in your
phone's notification settings.

## Gameplay statistics and crash reports

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, «App name» uses
**Google Firebase**: Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics.
They receive:

- gameplay events, such as levels reached and lost, how long a level took,
  «the game's own actions», coins earned and spent in the game, daily tasks,
  Rewards Boxes, Bell Quest progress, and reminder settings;
- advertising events: which ads were offered, shown or watched, the ad
  revenue each one earned, and technical errors when an ad fails to load;
- an app-instance identifier that Firebase creates for this installation (it
  is not your name, email or phone number, and resets if you reinstall), and
  your device's advertising ID, which links ad revenue to gameplay;
- device information, such as model, operating system, language and app
  version, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your
  IP address;
- crash reports and error details if the app crashes or misbehaves.

We use this only to improve the game: to see where levels are too hard, which
features are used, how ads perform, and what is going wrong. We do not use it
to identify you. Google processes it for us under its terms:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically.

## Advertising

«App name» is free and supported by ads. There are two kinds:

- **Optional ads** that you choose to watch for an in-game reward, such as
  «your game's reward», or opening the Rewards Box early.
- **Ad breaks.** Past level 10, a short ad plays after every few minutes of
  play, announced first by an "Ad break" screen.

Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**. To have ads ready when needed, the app
requests them from Google in the background while you play, not only at the
moment one is shown. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect and
process information from your device, such as:

- your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
- device information, such as model, operating system and language;
- your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
- information about the ads you see and interact with;
- technical and diagnostic information about ad performance.

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and, where
permitted, to show ads based on your interests. How Google handles this
information is described in its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not sell your information for money.

## Privacy choices and consent

Where the law requires it, «App name» uses Google's consent tool (the User
Messaging Platform) to show you a privacy message before personalised ads are
used, and to record your choice. Which message you see, if any, depends on
where you are.

## Your choices

- **Interest-based ads.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt
  out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings under Google → Ads (or
  Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone). You will still see ads, but they
  will not be based on your interests.
- **United States residents.** Some state laws treat sharing device data for
  interest-based advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.
  You can opt out in the game: **Settings → Do not sell or share my personal
  information**, which opens Google's privacy choices. This option appears
  where Google's consent tool determines it applies to you.
- **Reminders.** Sent only if you allow notifications; switch them off any
  time in Settings.
- **Stop everything.** Uninstalling the app stops all collection. To have
  statistics already sent deleted, contact us at «contact email»
  with the approximate date you installed the game.
